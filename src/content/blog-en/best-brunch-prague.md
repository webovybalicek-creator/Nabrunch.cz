<p>Prague has become one of Europe's best cities for brunch. Specialty coffee, sourdough bakeries and creative breakfast menus have spread far beyond the tourist centre. We are a Czech breakfast guide and <strong>we have visited every place on this list ourselves</strong>. Here are our 15 favourites, sorted by neighbourhood, so you can pick one close to where you're staying.</p>

<h2>Old Town and centre</h2>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/traum-concept-praha/">Traum Concept</a> – Berlin vibes near Náměstí Republiky</h3>
<p>A new brunch bistro with raw walls and neon, run by chefs from Eska and Michelin-recommended Štangl. Turkish eggs with lime labneh, hashbrown with gravlax and <strong>crème brûlée French toast</strong>. No reservations.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/dabl-bistro-praha/">Dabl.</a> – behind Kotva</h3>
<p>Tiramisu French toast, sourdough pancakes with pistachio and kadaif and a hidden courtyard – a non-touristy spot five minutes from Old Town Square.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/stranou-praha/">Stranou</a> – Malá Strana</h3>
<p>A vaulted bistro by the Petřín funicular with a seasonal breakfast menu until 15:00. Perfect before or after Charles Bridge.</p>

<h2>Karlín</h2>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/eska-karlin/">Eska</a></h3>
<p>An icon of Prague's brunch scene: a bakery with an open kitchen in an industrial space, house sourdough and seasonal Czech ingredients. A must for food lovers.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/spojka-karlin/">Spojka</a></h3>
<p>A stylish Karlín favourite for breakfast and long weekend brunches, with vegan options.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/dvorek-karlin-praha/">Dvorek</a></h3>
<p>A relaxed bistro on Křižíkova street with a slow weekend brunch (9:00–14:00) – a local secret.</p>

<h2>Holešovice and Letná</h2>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/vnitroblock-praha/">Vnitroblock</a></h3>
<p>An industrial hall with its own roastery and bakery. Weekend brunch until 15:00 – try the scrambled eggs with <strong>fresh black truffle</strong>.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/phills-corner-praha/">Phill's Corner</a></h3>
<p>Waffles with bacon and maple syrup, eggs in a glass with hollandaise and the Super pancake with salmon. Opens at 7:30 on weekdays.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/matilda-praha/">Matilda</a></h3>
<p>An Italian morning in Letná: maritozzi cream buns, espresso and cacio e pepe scrambled eggs.</p>

<h2>Vinohrady</h2>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/cafefin-praha/">Cafefin</a></h3>
<p>A brunch classic on Náměstí Jiřího z Poděbrad – shakshuka, toasts with figs or burrata and porridge with caramelised banana.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/srnky-americka-praha/">Srnky Americká</a></h3>
<p>All-day brunch near Náměstí Míru, famous for <strong>lívance</strong> – thick Czech pancakes – made from farm ingredients.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/vila-63-praha/">Vila 63</a></h3>
<p>Czech breakfast in a 1920s villa with a garden. Hemenex (ham and eggs), sausages with horseradish and a trio of spreads.</p>

<h2>Žižkov and Vršovice</h2>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/seiferts-brunch-praha/">Seifert's Brunch</a></h3>
<p>"As Czech as it gets": eggs, Prague ham from the butcher next door, Blaťácké zlato cheese and Bread Society sourdough.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/flat-cafe-praha/">FLAT</a></h3>
<p>A café styled like a British living room, with a proper English breakfast (also vegan) and coffee from London's Square Mile.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/sestava-vrsovice/">Sestava</a></h3>
<p>A neighbourhood café and roastery run by six friends – eggs, lívance and sourdough toasts from 7:00.</p>

<h2>Brunch tips for Prague</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Weekends are busy.</strong> Arrive before 10:00 or book where possible – many popular places don't take reservations.</li>
<li><strong>Prices:</strong> a brunch dish usually costs 200–350 CZK, a flat white 70–95 CZK.</li>
<li><strong>Try Czech classics</strong> – lívance, koláče, Prague ham or a větrník cream puff.</li>
<li><strong>Tip around 10 %</strong> for table service; cards are accepted almost everywhere.</li>
</ul>
