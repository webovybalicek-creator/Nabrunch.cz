<p>Holešovice and Letná, across the river north of the Old Town, are Prague's creative quarter – home to the DOX art centre, the National Gallery's Trade Fair Palace, Prague Market and Letná Park with its famous view of the bridges. The breakfast scene here is one of the best in the city.</p>

<h2>Holešovice</h2>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/vnitroblock-praha/">Vnitroblock</a></h3>
<p>A former factory with a roastery and bakery. Scrambled eggs with black truffle, Turkish eggs and blueberry pancakes; weekend brunch until 15:00.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/phills-corner-praha/">Phill's Corner</a></h3>
<p>Mornings are for breakfast here – from 7:30 on weekdays. Waffles with bacon and maple syrup, eggs in a glass with hollandaise.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/kafe-francin-praha/">Kafe Francin</a></h3>
<p>A café and roastery near the Trade Fair Palace with an English breakfast, croque madame and great value.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/la-forme-holesovice-praha/">La Forme Holešovice</a></h3>
<p>One of Prague's best bakeries – French toast with caramelised peaches, quark pancakes and filled croissants.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/supernova-bakehouse-holesovice-praha/">Supernova Bakehouse</a></h3>
<p>The Holešovice branch of the Karlín bakery, with a full breakfast menu – house croissants, the Supernova breakfast and milky rice with cinnamon bun crumble.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/mama-shelter-praha/">Mama Shelter</a></h3>
<p>Sunday all-you-can-eat brunch buffet, 12:00–15:00.</p>

<h2>Letná</h2>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/matilda-praha/">Matilda</a></h3>
<p>Italian breakfast with maritozzi and cacio e pepe scrambled eggs; garden seating.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/cafe-letka-praha/">Café Letka</a></h3>
<p>A cosy café near Letná Park with Five Elephant coffee and an outstanding French toast.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/und-kaffee-praha/">Ünd</a></h3>
<p>Laminated pastry and cardamom swirls from the Gemüse Corner founders, daily from 8:00.</p>

<p>After breakfast, walk through Letná Park to the metronome viewpoint, or cross Stromovka park towards Prague Zoo.</p>
