<p>Karlín, a former industrial district just east of the centre (two metro stops from Náměstí Republiky, station Křižíkova), is now Prague's most exciting neighbourhood for food. Wide streets, converted factories and dozens of cafés make it ideal for a brunch crawl. These are our favourites.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/eska-karlin/">Eska</a> – bakery and restaurant in a factory</h3>
<p>An icon of Prague's brunch scene: a bakery with an open kitchen in an industrial space, house sourdough, seasonal Czech ingredients and original breakfasts. Weekdays from 8:00.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/spojka-karlin/">Spojka</a> – all-day bistro on Karlínské náměstí</h3>
<p>A stylish space with quality food, vegan options and a relaxed atmosphere. Open until late.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/dvorek-karlin-praha/">Dvorek</a> – courtyard bistro</h3>
<p>Weekday lunches for the local offices, then a slow weekend brunch (9:00–14:00) with seasonal ingredients and specialty coffee.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/bistro-proti-proudu-praha/">Bistro Proti Proudu</a></h3>
<p>An Italian-inspired bistro near Florenc, open from 7:30 on weekdays; breakfast until noon.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/etapa-karlin-praha/">Etapa</a></h3>
<p>Colourful, vegetable-forward seasonal dishes and 100% wholegrain sourdough baked in-house – a top pick for vegetarians.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/supernova-bakehouse-karlin-praha/">Supernova Bakehouse</a></h3>
<p>Two-tone pistachio croissants, salted caramel croissants and cinnamon buns with pistachio cream, in a photogenic interior.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/nostress-bakery-karlin-praha/">Nostress Bakery</a></h3>
<p>A French bakery on Karlín Square – scrambled eggs, tartines, quiches and croissants. Mon–Sat from 8:00.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/the-oat-bar-praha/">The Oat Bar</a></h3>
<p>Healthy breakfast from 7:00 every day – great before work or a run along the Vltava.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/muj-salek-kavy-praha/">Můj šálek kávy</a></h3>
<p>A Karlín classic from the Doubleshot family – excellent coffee and a bold brunch menu with Turkish eggs and salsiccia.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/cafe-v-raji-praha/">Café v ráji</a></h3>
<p>An Art Nouveau family café on Karlín Square, open from 7:45 on weekdays, with piano concerts in the evening.</p>

<h2>Tips for Karlín</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Getting there:</strong> metro B to Křižíkova or Florenc, or trams along Sokolovská.</li>
<li>Combine brunch with a walk along the Vltava riverside or up to Vítkov Hill and its National Memorial.</li>
<li>At weekends, the most popular places fill up by 10:00.</li>
</ul>
