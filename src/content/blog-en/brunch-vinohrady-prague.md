<p>Vinohrady is one of Prague's most beautiful residential districts – tree-lined streets, Art Nouveau houses and two lively squares, <strong>Náměstí Míru</strong> and <strong>Náměstí Jiřího z Poděbrad</strong> (with its Saturday farmers' market). It's also packed with great breakfast cafés. Here are our picks.</p>

<h2>Around Jiřího z Poděbrad</h2>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/cafefin-praha/">Cafefin</a></h3>
<p>Right on the square: shakshuka, toasts with figs or burrata and porridge with caramelised banana. A Prague brunch institution.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/pomalu-pomalu-praha/">Pomalu pomalu</a></h3>
<p>New in autumn 2026 from the Kin &amp; K Bakery team – sourdough, cardamom knots and seasonal breakfast plates.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/tisse-bakery-praha/">TISSE Bakery</a></h3>
<p>A craft bakery with slow-fermented bread, lots of vegan pastry and a famous pistachio croissant.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/meme-brunch-praha/">Mèmè &amp; Brunch</a></h3>
<p>Creative brunch plates and a black sesame matcha latte you won't forget.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/spizirna-1902-praha/">Spižírna 1902</a></h3>
<p>Café, bistro and bakery with a seasonal menu; weekend brunch 9:00–16:00.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/antoninovo-pekarstvi/">Antonínovo pekařství</a></h3>
<p>A traditional Czech bakery open daily from 7:00 – try makovec (poppy seed cake) or perník (gingerbread).</p>

<h2>Around Náměstí Míru</h2>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/srnky-americka-praha/">Srnky Americká</a></h3>
<p>All-day brunch with famous lívance (Czech pancakes) from farm ingredients. Weekdays from 7:30.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/bistro-cafe-laskava/">Laskavá</a></h3>
<p>A romantic brick-walled café with all-day breakfast and a courtyard garden.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/o-mai-coffee-brunch-praha/">O-Mai Coffee &amp; Brunch</a></h3>
<p>Avocado toasts, vegetarian options and creative matcha drinks.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/yorkmut-cafe-praha/">Yorkmut Café</a></h3>
<p>A dog café – coffee and baked sandwiches with friendly resident dogs.</p>

<h2>Towards Vršovice</h2>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/vila-63-praha/">Vila 63</a></h3>
<p>Czech breakfast in a 1920s villa with a garden, Wednesday to Sunday.</p>
