<p>Žižkov is Prague's bohemian district – famous for having more pubs per head than anywhere in Europe and for the futuristic <strong>Žižkov TV Tower</strong>. It's less polished than Vinohrady next door, but that's exactly its charm: honest cafés, good prices and very few tourists.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/seiferts-brunch-praha/">Seifert's Brunch</a></h3>
<p>A family bistro named after Nobel-winning poet Jaroslav Seifert, who grew up in Žižkov. Czech-style brunch every day – eggs, Prague ham, Blaťácké zlato cheese and sourdough.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/sladka-cesta-praha/">Sladká cesta</a></h3>
<p>House-baked bagels, brioches and koláče, all-day breakfast from 7:00 on weekdays – and prices from 69 CZK.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/dobroteka-zizkov-praha/">Dobrotéka</a></h3>
<p>Café and deli with one of the most affordable brunches in Prague (until 13:00) and a covered garden.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/soma-praha/">SOMA</a></h3>
<p>Specialty coffee from Czech and European roasters and a seasonal brunch menu. Don't miss the pumpkin bread.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/golden-egg-zizkov-praha/">Golden Egg</a></h3>
<p>An egg-focused bistro near the main railway station – eggs Benedict with salmon or ham, omelettes and egg brioche sandwiches, all with free-range eggs. Weekdays from 8:00.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/sensu-cafe-praha/">Sensu Cafe</a></h3>
<p>A matcha specialist near Želivského metro – iced matcha with vanilla cloud and matcha cakes.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/spizirna-1902-praha/">Spižírna 1902</a></h3>
<p>On the border with Vinohrady – breakfast on weekdays, brunch 9:00–16:00 at weekends.</p>

<h2>Tips for Žižkov</h2>
<ul>
<li>Combine breakfast with the <strong>TV Tower observation deck</strong> or a walk up Vítkov Hill.</li>
<li>Žižkov is hilly – trams 5, 9 and 15 run along Seifertova street.</li>
</ul>
