<p>Many Prague cafés don't open until 9:00 – no use if you have an early train, a flight or want to see Charles Bridge before the crowds. These places serve <strong>good coffee and breakfast from 7:00 or 7:30</strong>. Always check the hours on each place's page, especially at weekends.</p>

<h2>Open from 7:00</h2>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/the-oat-bar-praha/">The Oat Bar</a> – Karlín</h3>
<p>Healthy breakfast from 7:00, every day of the week.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/55-bakery-praha/">55 Bakery</a> – Smíchov</h3>
<p>Pistachio croissants and cinnamon rolls from 7:00 daily, near Anděl.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/antoninovo-pekarstvi/">Antonínovo pekařství</a> – Vinohrady</h3>
<p>A Czech bakery open daily from 7:00, by Náměstí Jiřího z Poděbrad.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/sladka-cesta-praha/">Sladká cesta</a> – Žižkov</h3>
<p>All-day breakfast from 7:00 on weekdays, close to the main railway station.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/sestava-vrsovice/">Sestava</a> – Vršovice</h3>
<p>Own-roasted coffee and breakfast from 7:00 on weekdays.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/bistro-b-praha/">Bistro B</a> – Budějovická metro</h3>
<p>Eggs Benedict right at the metro exit from 7:00 on weekdays.</p>

<h2>Open from 7:30</h2>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/la-forme-rytirska-praha/">La Forme</a> – Old Town</h3>
<p>Filled croissants three minutes from Old Town Square, from 7:30 on weekdays.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/stranou-praha/">Stranou</a> – Malá Strana</h3>
<p>Seasonal breakfast by the Petřín funicular, from 7:30 on weekdays.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/hans-coffee-praha/">Hans Coffee</a> – Náměstí Republiky</h3>
<p>La Cabra coffee and cardamom buns, weekdays from 7:30.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/phills-corner-praha/">Phill's Corner</a> and <a href="/en/places/kafe-francin-praha/">Kafe Francin</a> – Holešovice</h3>
<p>Two great breakfast spots, both opening at 7:30 on weekdays.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/srnky-americka-praha/">Srnky Americká</a> – Vinohrady</h3>
<p>Czech pancakes from 7:30 on weekdays.</p>

<h2>Near the main railway station</h2>
<p><a href="/en/places/foyer-cafe-praha/">Foyer Café</a>, inside the Art Nouveau station building, opens at 8:00 every day – croissants and coffee with a view of the trains.</p>
