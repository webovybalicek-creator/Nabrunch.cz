<p>Sunday is brunch day in Prague. Some hotels and restaurants put on big <strong>all-you-can-eat buffets</strong>, while neighbourhood cafés serve brunch à la carte from 9:00. Here's where to go – whether you want a long celebration with prosecco or a relaxed plate of eggs.</p>

<h2>Sunday brunch buffets</h2>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/mama-shelter-praha/">Mama Shelter</a> – Holešovice</h3>
<p>The colourful design hotel's <strong>Mama Brunch</strong> runs every Sunday 12:00–15:00. Around 990 CZK includes the buffet, a welcome drink and soft drinks. Big dessert table, relaxed crowd. Book by phone.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/kafkoff-praha/">Kafkoff</a> – Old Town Square</h3>
<p>Brunch, Beats &amp; Kids every Sunday 11:00–15:00: buffet with salmon tartare and a pancake bar, <strong>bottomless prosecco</strong>, a DJ and a full kids' programme with a bouncy castle. Around 1,190 CZK for adults.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/buddha-bar-praha/">Buddha-Bar</a> – Old Town</h3>
<p>The most luxurious option: sushi, fresh oysters, a noodle bar and desserts under a giant Buddha statue. Sundays 12:00–16:00 from October to April, around 1,599 CZK, kids under 5 free.</p>

<h2>Cafés for a slow Sunday morning</h2>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/spizirna-1902-praha/">Spižírna 1902</a> – Vinohrady</h3>
<p>Weekend brunch 9:00–16:00 with a seasonal menu and cakes from their own bakery. No weekend bookings – arrive at 9:00.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/seiferts-brunch-praha/">Seifert's Brunch</a> – Žižkov</h3>
<p>Czech-style brunch until 18:00 on weekends – one of the longest brunch windows in town.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/vnitroblock-praha/">Vnitroblock</a> – Holešovice</h3>
<p>Weekend breakfast 9:00–15:00 in an industrial hall, with coffee from its own roastery.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/cafe-letka-praha/">Café Letka</a> – Letná</h3>
<p>French toast with poached pear and brown butter, then a Sunday walk in Letná Park.</p>

<h2>Good to know</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Book buffets in advance</strong> – they fill up days ahead, especially around holidays.</li>
<li>Many small cafés <strong>don't take weekend reservations</strong>; come early or expect a short wait.</li>
<li>On Czech public holidays some places close – check opening hours on each place's page.</li>
</ul>
