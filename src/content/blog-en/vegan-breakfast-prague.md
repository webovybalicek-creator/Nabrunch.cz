<p>Prague is surprisingly easy for vegans. Plant-based milk is standard in almost every specialty café, and many brunch spots have proper vegan dishes, not just a side salad. These are the places where vegans and vegetarians can eat well at breakfast.</p>

<h2>Cafés with strong vegan menus</h2>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/spojka-karlin/">Spojka</a> – Karlín</h3>
<p>A stylish Karlín bistro with plant-based breakfast options.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/etapa-karlin-praha/">Etapa</a> – Karlín</h3>
<p>Vegetable-forward seasonal cooking and wholegrain sourdough baked in-house.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/tisse-bakery-praha/">TISSE Bakery</a> – Vinohrady</h3>
<p>A craft bakery with a wide range of <strong>vegan pastry</strong> – rare in Prague.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/kocici-kavarna-praha/">Kavárna Kočičí</a> – Old Town</h3>
<p>A cat café near Old Town Square with mostly vegan and vegetarian dishes, including raw and gluten-free treats.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/flat-cafe-praha/">FLAT</a> – Vršovice</h3>
<p>Serves a full <strong>vegan English breakfast</strong>.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/cafefin-praha/">Cafefin</a> – Vinohrady</h3>
<p>Plenty of vegetarian and vegan options alongside the brunch classics.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/smetanaq-cafe-bistro-praha/">SmetanaQ</a> – riverside, Old Town</h3>
<p>Vegan options with a view of the Vltava and Prague Castle.</p>

<h3>👉🏼 <a href="/en/places/the-oat-bar-praha/">The Oat Bar</a> – Karlín</h3>
<p>Healthy breakfasts from 7:00 – oat-based and easy to keep plant-based.</p>

<h2>Useful Czech words</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>veganské</strong> – vegan, <strong>vegetariánské</strong> – vegetarian</li>
<li><strong>bez mléka</strong> – without milk, <strong>rostlinné mléko</strong> – plant-based milk</li>
<li><strong>bez vajec</strong> – without eggs, <strong>bezlepkové</strong> – gluten-free</li>
</ul>
